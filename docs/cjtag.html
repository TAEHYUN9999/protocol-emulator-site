firmware/cjtag.pe A cJTAG target on two wires (TCKC, TMSC) in front of a 4-wire IEEE 1149.1 TAP: counts TMSC edges while TCKC is high (reset, selection, deselection escapes), takes the activation code after a selection escape, then turns each OScan1 packet (nTDI, TMS, TDO) into one TCK clock of the TAP and drives the TAP's TDO back on TMSC in the third cycle. One hart. 116 of 512
firmware/cjtag.pe + firmware/jtag_tap.pe One-chip loopback: the two files one after the other; hart 0 runs the adapter, hart 1 the TAP target of docs/jtag_tap.md (IDCODE, BYPASS, USER), the two joined by the TAP pins P0..P3 inside the chip. A cJTAG probe on P4/P5 scans the TAP. 384 of 512 (hart 1 starts at word 116)

No RTL change and no change to jtag_tap.pe: existing instructions only (waitp, rdp polling with test, shin/shin.lsb, high/low/rel, tset/twait for the reset pulses, ld/st). Checked in simulation only, not on a board.

Sources

IEEE Std 1149.7 was not read (IEEE sells it). The rules come from the tools and vendor documents that implement it:

Source Used for
OpenOCD src/jtag/drivers/ftdi.c (commit 4900bcdf, 2026-11-10), cjtag_reset_online_activate() and oscan1_mpsse_clock_*() the activation a real probe sends: TCKC 0 and TMSC 0, reset escape of 8 TMSC edges with TCKC high, 3 TCKC pulses, selection escape of 6 edges, then OAC 1100 ("2 wires"), EC 1000, CP 0000 written bit 0 first (in time 0,0,1,1 0,0,0,1 0,0,0,0), each set while TCKC is low and taken at the rising edge; OScan1 = 3 TCK cycles per TAP clock: TMSC = the negation of TDI, then TMS, then a cycle with TMSC let go for TDO; the probe changes TMSC on the falling edge and reads on the rising edge
SEGGER knowledge base "J-Link cJTAG specifics" (2024) reset escape: 8 or more TMSC changes while TCK is high (J-Link sends 10); selection escape 7; short activation 0Ch, 08h, 00h in 4 bits each (the same code, bit 0 first); the target releases TMSC on the rising edge of TCKC and a keeper holds the level driven since the falling edge
TI CC2538 user's guide SWRU319C, Table 6-1; TI CC13x0/CC26x0 TRM SWCU117I, 5.2 and Table 5-3 the chip drives TMSC only while TCK is low; OScan1 packet nTDI, TMS, TDO; TMSC has a pull-up, TCK a pull-down
TI, "An Introduction to IEEE P1149.7" (SWDFT 2009) an escape is TMSC toggling while TCK is 1
Rule Adapter
Escape = TMSC edges while TCKC is high; reset 8 or more, selection 6 or 7 counted in every TCKC high phase; 8 or more: reset, 6 or 7: selection
Deselection 4 or 5 edges taken from the usual description of 1149.7; no public text found to confirm it
Activation after a selection escape: OAC, EC, CP, 12 bits taken at TCKC rising edges compared with 0011 0001 0000 (time order); anything else is refused
OScan1: nTDI, TMS, TDO; probe changes TMSC on the falling edge TMSC read at each rising edge; TDI is the complement of the first bit
The target drives TDO only while TCKC is low, lets go at the rising edge drives from the falling edge that starts the third cycle (after reading TDO from the TAP), lets go 6 clocks after the rising edge
One TAP clock per packet TCK rises right after the third rising edge and falls with TCKC

Choices not taken from a source: the reset escape also takes the TAP behind to Test-Logic-Reset (5 TCK pulses with TMS high; OpenOCD's comment says a TAP reset happens with it); 1 to 3 edges are no escape but a broken TCKC cycle, and the adapter goes offline; the selection escape needs the activation code every time (no "JScan0" 4-wire pass-through).

Pins

Pin Use
P4 TCKC, input
P5 TMSC, input; driven by the adapter only in the TDO cycle (from the falling edge to just after the rising edge). Needs a keeper (or a pull-up, at a slower TCKC)
P0, P1, P2 TCK, TMS, TDI to the TAP (outputs of hart 0)
P3 TDO from the TAP (driven by hart 1 in Shift-xR, pulled up otherwise)

The P0..P3 numbers are those of jtag_tap.pe and jtag.pe; each pin has one writer, so the two harts share them inside the chip (docs/jtag_tap.md, "Two harts on one chip").

Timing

The adapter follows TCKC with waitp, reads TMSC at the rising edge with shin, and then polls TCKC and TMSC with rdp every 6 ticks until TCKC falls, counting TMSC changes; a probe must hold each escape level for more than 6 ticks (240 ns; the tests use 1 us). In the third cycle it reads TDO about 4 ticks after it sees the falling edge, drives TMSC, lets go 6 clocks after the rising edge, raises TCK and lowers it after the falling edge. The TAP therefore sees TCK high for about half a TCKC period and low for two and a half, with TMS and TDI set one and two TCKC cycles before the rise.

Parameter Value
TCKC right at 250 and 500 kHz; wrong at 833 kHz and 1 MHz (the probe reads TDO before the adapter drives it). Before the pad rule of branch int3 (revisions) 833 kHz was right: the rule enables a push-pull high from release one clock after the instruction (dual_role.md), the keeper holds TMSC low after a 0, so a TDO of 1 reaches the line one clock later, and at 833 kHz that clock is past the probe's sampling edge
TMSC drive by the chip from a low TCKC only; let go 6 clocks after the rising edge
Escape toggles at least 240 ns apart
TAP reset after a reset escape 5 TCK pulses of 6 + 6 ticks

Tests

test/test_cjtag.py, run from a private directory:

T=<repo>/test; R=$T/sim_build/<name>; mkdir -p $R; cd $R
PYTHONPATH=$T make -B -f $T/Makefile PWD=$T SIM_BUILD=$R/build COCOTB_TEST_MODULES=test_cjtag

The probe model is written from the sources above, not from the firmware: OpenOCD's activation sequence, escapes with any number of edges, OScan1 packets built from (TMS, TDI) TAP clocks, a keeper on TMSC that holds the last level whenever nobody drives, and a record of every TMSC change with the chip's TMSC enable and the TCKC level. The TAP is checked as a 1149.1 TAP (state tracking, IR capture ending in 01, IDCODE with bit 0 = 1, BYPASS one bit), as in test_jtag_tap.py. Only pins and data memory addresses come from the firmware headers.

The results and wall times are those of the full regression of branch int5e (tree of 2f8eae4, 2026-11-13). Branch int3 (the pad rule of dual_role.md; run on branch int3fix, whose src/ is that of 0a07d60) gave the same three results. Before that rule test_cjtag_speed was right at 500 and 833 kHz and wrong at 1 MHz, and the same test on the int3 RTL without the first half of the rule is right at 833 kHz again: the clock the rule adds to a TDO of 1 driven from release (the keeper holding a 0) is what 833 kHz lacks.

Limits

  • Only OScan1, and only through the short activation (OAC/EC/CP after a selection escape); no JScan0..3 formats, no TAP.7 commands by zero-bit DR scans, no other OScan formats, no TCKC pause states or custom escapes.
  • The escape counts for deselection (4, 5) are not from a public text; reset and selection follow OpenOCD and J-Link.
  • TCKC up to 500 kHz since the pad rule of branch int3 (also on int5e; 833 kHz before that rule); 1149.7 allows far more.
  • One TAP behind the adapter (no star topology, no TAP.7 controller addressing).
  • Simulation only: no board, no gate-level run.
