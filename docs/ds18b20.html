File Role Words
firmware/ds18b20.pe The chip answers on a 1-Wire bus as a DS18B20: reset and presence, Read/Match/Skip/Search ROM and Alarm Search, Read/Write/Copy Scratchpad, Recall E2, Read Power Supply, and Convert T with the conversion time of the chosen resolution, during which read slots return 0. Hart 0 is the bus, hart 1 times the conversions and takes the temperature from the host. 302 of 512 (hart 0 268, hart 1 34)

The design already had a 1-Wire master (firmware/onewire.pe); this is the other side. Both run on one chip in test_ds18b20_loopback_onewire (401 words). No RTL change. Checked in simulation only.

Reference: DS18B20 "Programmable Resolution 1-Wire Digital Thermometer", Dallas Semiconductor, preliminary data sheet (www.dalsemi.com): the AC table, Table 2 (temperature format, power-on 0550h), Figure 7 and Table 3 (configuration, conversion times), Figure 8 (memory map), the ROM and function command descriptions and Table 4. Search procedure: Maxim AN187. (The current Analog Devices data sheet could not be downloaded here; where the two may differ it is noted below.)

Pins

Pin Use
uio[0] (P0) DQ, open drain (OWP sets another pin). Needs a pull-up. The chip is externally powered: Read Power Supply answers 1

Bus timing

Data sheet Firmware
Reset seen master low tRSTL >= 480 us a low of 300 us or more (a slot is at most 120 us)
Presence tPDHIGH 15 to 60 us, then low tPDLOW 60 to 240 us 30 us, then 120 us low
Write slot sampled a slave samples 15 to 60 us after the falling edge 30 us after it (the chip's input synchronizer adds 2 clocks)
Read slot, sending 0 the master samples before tRDV = 15 us low from the falling edge (about 100 ns after it) to 30 us

Every slot is one routine: wait for the falling edge, put the bit on the line at once (shout.lsb.od), sample 30 us after the edge, release, and wait for the line to rise with a 270 us timeout that means a reset pulse. Commands that read or write bytes call it with a bit count (1 or 8).

Commands

Command Behaviour
33 Read ROM family 28h, serial (data memory 1 to 6), CRC-8 of the 7 bytes
55 Match ROM all 64 bits must match, otherwise the chip waits for the next reset
CC Skip ROM
F0 Search ROM, EC Alarm Search per ROM bit (LSB first): the bit, its complement, then the master's bit; a different bit drops the chip out until the next reset. Alarm Search only while the alarm flag is set
4E Write Scratchpad TH, TL, configuration (bits 0 to 4 read 1, bit 7 reads 0, Figure 7)
BE Read Scratchpad 9 bytes, the last the CRC of the first 8 (kept up to date whenever the scratchpad changes, so the first byte goes out at once)
48 Copy Scratchpad TH, TL, configuration to E2; read slots then answer 1 (no E2 write time)
B8 Recall E2 E2 to the scratchpad; read slots answer 1
B4 Read Power Supply read slots answer 1 (external supply)
44 Convert T the result is formed at once and copied to the scratchpad when the conversion time has passed; until then read slots answer 0, then 1

Conversion time by resolution: 75, 150, 300, 600 ms for 9, 10, 11, 12 bits (80 % of tCONV max: 93.75, 187.5, 375, 750 ms), in periods of 2.5 ms on hart 1. Lower resolutions clear the insignificant bits ("insignificant bits will contain zeros"; the current data sheet calls them undefined). Alarm: after each conversion, set when the result is higher than TH or lower than TL of the E2, as integer degrees (bits 11 to 4); the current data sheet says "lower than or equal to TL or higher than or equal to TH", the preliminary one used here says "higher than TH or lower than TL". Scratchpad bytes 5 to 7, which "will not read out any predictable pattern", are FFh, 0Ch, 10h (the values of real parts).

The temperature comes from the host: two bytes in the FIFO (high byte first, the 12-bit format of Table 2); the next conversion takes the latest pair. Power-on: the scratchpad holds 0550h (+85 C) and the E2 values.

Data memory

Address Contents
0 to 7 ROM: 28h, serial (1 to 6), CRC (computed at the start)
8 to 16 scratchpad (16 = its CRC)
17 to 19 E2: TH, TL, configuration
20, 21 sensor input
22, 23, 24 conversion request, busy, alarm
25 MAGIC: D5h when the host has written the serial and the E2 bytes (DATA_W); otherwise the start sets serial 01 02 03 04 05 06, TH 75 C, TL 70 C, configuration 7Fh
26 to 28 the result waiting for the conversion end

LOOP = 1 (assembler constant): the conversion ends at once and hart 1 is not used, for running next to another firmware on hart 0.

Tests

test/test_ds18b20.py, run from a private directory:

T=<repo>/test; R=$T/sim_build/<name>; mkdir -p $R; cd $R
PYTHONPATH=$T make -B -f $T/Makefile PWD=$T SIM_BUILD=$R/build COCOTB_TEST_MODULES=test_ds18b20

The master model is written from the data sheet, not from the firmware: it measures tPDHIGH and tPDLOW of every presence pulse, samples read slots at 14 us and checks the line is free again by 59.5 us, and runs the AN187 search. Other devices on the line are the DS18B20 models of test/test_onewire.py. Conversions run with the hart 1 time base divided by 100 except in test_ds18b20_realtime_conversion.

Limits

  • External power only: no parasite-power behaviour (strong pull-up), Read Power Supply always answers 1.
  • No overdrive speed.
  • Copy Scratchpad and Recall E2 take no time; the E2 is data memory (kept over a soft reset, not a power cycle).
  • A Read Scratchpad during a conversion can see the result being copied in (hart 1 writes bytes 0, 1 and the CRC while hart 0 may be sending).
  • The temperature is whatever the host sends; no sensor.
