Revisions: which RTL a result belongs to

The design grew in steps, and not every check was repeated after every step. This page names the steps of the two-core RTL, says what was run on each, and lists the hardening runs, so that every number of the README and of docs/ can be traced to the RTL it was measured on. The steps before the two-core RTL (the 256-word and 512-word designs, adv, cap2, int1 to int4) are in verification.md, Revisions.

The regressions of the step with the delay lines

Every module of commit 80f6218 ran whole on that commit, and no test failed: 130 modules, 715 tests. The tree changed after it: test_dly_train was added and test files came in with later merges. All 131 modules of that later tree ran whole on commit 2f8eae4 (branch int5e; its src/ differs from that of 80f6218 only in the place of two wire declarations): 721 tests, 718 pass, 2 skipped, 1 fail. The two skipped tests are the SDF-only ones of test_sdf. The failure is test_can_gen_rates: the test drives the CAN node up to rates at which it is late by design, its own checks passed, and the decorator that reads the late flag at the end of every test failed it. Commit bea5db6 marks that test and test_can_gen_limit as late on purpose, and the module passes 8 of 8 on that tree; the 131 modules were not run again together. So the last run in which every test passes is that of 80f6218, and the last run of every module is that of 2f8eae4, with one failure that is explained and fixed in the test but not rerun in a whole regression. Neither is a run of a later step. In the regression before the delay lines three tests of one module failed, all in test_reset_release: its expected values had been written for the RTL without a reset synchronizer. The test file was changed (no RTL change) and that module passed alone, 4 of 4, and again in the regression of 80f6218 (int5.md). The logs, results and summaries are in evidence/.

Hardening runs

All runs use the Tiny Tapeout IHP flow (LibreLane) locally in its container, three corners, clock 20 ns. The flow setup of runs H4d and H5 is not the one of src/config.json in this tree: they read src/pe_dly.sdc as a user SDC (Tiny Tapeout has not said whether it accepts one), and raise the setup slack margin of the repair steps to 3.5 ns (dly.md, Hardening). tools/hard_gate.py checks a run against its committed setup and a list of physical checks the flow does not stop on.

"SDF in three corners" in this table is a simulation with the SDF delays. Hold is not shown by these runs. Icarus does not apply the timing checks of the SDF or of the cell models, so a flip-flop takes a new value whenever it arrives before the clock edge in the simulation, and a hold violation of a few hundred picoseconds goes unseen. Hold is judged by the signoff STA of the hardening run (tools/hard_gate.py, item [8]). Control (2026-11-18): hardening F5k failed signoff with 58 hold violations (39 at the fast corner, 18 typical, 1 slow; worst -0.196 ns, fast; the summary.rpt of step 57-openroad-stapostpnr in the run of worktree int6_full5k_dev4), yet its final netlist (MD5 89336d7d) passed the fast-corner SDF runs: test/test.py 19 of 19 and test_usb_fs_fine_sdf 1 of 1, no SDF error (summary.txt of test/sim_build/sdf in worktree int6_f5k_simv, SRC_REV a915ba3).

SRAM read data in the SDF runs of F5t-d66 and later (found 2026-11-19). The SRAM macro models have two module paths to A_DOUT, one from A_CLK and one from A_BIST_CLK, both 1.0 ns until annotated; the liberty, and so the flow's SDF, times A_DOUT from A_CLK only. Up to H5 A_BIST_CLK was tied low; c54055d (the SRAM self test connected, 2026-11-14) gave it the clock and 8c98be2 (2026-11-17) a leaf of the same SRAM clock tree as A_CLK. Icarus takes the delay of the path whose input changed last, so in the SDF runs of the netlists since then (F5t-d66, a4861cf, 2ad37f2) a macro whose BIST clock edge arrived after its A_CLK edge gave its read data 1.0 ns after the clock, the model's default, instead of the SDF delay (fast 2.3, typ 3.8, slow 6.3 ns). On 2ad37f2 test_sdf_sram_clock_to_output measured 1,000 ps at u_shm1 and u_capmem in the fast corner and at all three 512-word macros in the typical and slow corners; the two 1024-word program memories matched the SDF. Those runs were therefore optimistic on the read paths of those macros. Setup is judged by STA. The XSim hold check has the same two paths with no A_BIST_CLK entry in its SDF; it measured A_CLK to A_DOUT of u_capmem as 1 ps on these netlists against 2,306 ps on 89336d7d, before the change, so its read data came early, the conservative side for hold, and it is not repeated. Since 25e0297 test/sdf_for_icarus.py gives A_BIST_CLK to A_DOUT the A_CLK values, so the output follows the later of the two edges, and it fails if a macro lacks either entry. The F5t-d66 runs are not repeated. Those of 2ad37f2 were run again with it on 2026-11-19 (worktree d66ant_sdfre_simv at 8c9e3c0): test/test.py 19 of 19 and test_usb_fs_fine_sdf 1 of 1 in each corner, with the same edge errors as before, and test/test_sdf.py 5 of 5 (row of 2ad37f2).
