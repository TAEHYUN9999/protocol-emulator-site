The protocols the brief names, one by one

What the chip does for each protocol the competition brief names, the tests behind it, what ran on a board, and the limits. The short form is in the README.

The "Tests" column counts the tests of the module in the last full regression, made on the two-core RTL with the delay lines (commit 80f6218); all of them pass there. "Gate level" in that column is a run on the netlist of the one-core RTL. The "Board" column is the chip RTL on a ZCU104 against a KD240 (docs/verification.md, section 9); every entry in it is a run of the one-core RTL or of an older RTL, except those marked "F5t-d66 RTL": runs of 2026-11-18 and 19 on bit ad022a41, built from the RTL of F5t (F5t-d66, the chip submitted before the area reduction, differs only in a placement setting).

UART uart_gen.pe: 5 to 8 data bits, parity, 1 or 2 stop bits, bit time from the data memory, full duplex on two harts; autobaud.pe test_uart 8/8; gate level 8/8 fixed UART with capture; general UART 8N1, 7E1, 6O1 full duplex down to 8 ticks a bit (320 ns, 3.125 Mbaud)
SPI master spi_master_gen.pe (modes 0 to 3, MSB or LSB first); device spi_device_gen.pe (modes 0 and 3, 128 registers); spi_device_pel.pe with the reply edge placed by the launcher; Quad SPI flash reads test_spi 8/8, also at gate level; test_device 8/8; test_pel 4/4 master; general master and device on two harts; device against Xilinx AXI Quad SPI under Linux (older RTL) master SCK up to 3.125 MHz; device up to 2.27 MHz for reads, 2.50 MHz for writes; device modes 1 and 2 not supported
I2C controller i2c_master_gen.pe (7- and 10-bit addresses, clock stretching, arbitration lost and NACK reported, bus clear); target i2c_target_gen.pe with stretching; passive sniffer; SMBus and PMBus on top test_i2c 12/12; test_i2c_sniff 4/4 master; general master in Fast-mode; target against Xilinx AXI IIC under Linux (older RTL) target 556 kHz without stretching, 1 MHz with it; the controller waits at most 2.6 ms for a stretched clock
USB low speed usb_dev.pe: a device that answers within the bus turnaround, checks and makes CRC5 and CRC16, detects bus reset and keep-alive test_usb_gen 9/9 against a host model written from USB 2.0 chapters 7 and 8; test_usb 6/6 F5t-d66 RTL: a PC (xHCI behind a hub) found the device 10 of 10 times; 8 reached configuration 1 with no host error, 1 after the host's own retry, 1 failed SET_CONFIGURATION (-75; the chip-side log shows a descriptor cut short, which points at the board host's Python feeder, not yet separated from the chip) (recordprivate repo file) logic level only: the pull-up and edge rates need parts outside the chip (on the board: 220 ohm in series, 1.5 kohm on D-, 220 pF). Full speed: usb_fs.pe on the line unit, test_usb_fs 10/10, simulation only on the two-core RTL; its edges sit on a 10 ns grid and do not meet the source jitter limit (FAQ 8)
10 Mbit Ethernet hardware 10BASE-T transmitter (Manchester, CRC-32, link pulses) and receiver (clock recovery, CRC check, address filter) on the frame buffer; net.pe answers ARP, ping and UDP echo by editing the frame in place test_eth 9/9, test_eth_rx 11/11 with jitter and clock offset, test_net 7/7, test_udp 3/3 F5t-d66 RTL: transmit to a PC network card, 50 of 50 UDP frames byte-exact in the PC's capture (a first run lost the first frame while the link came up); receive not run, its parts have not arrived (recordprivate repo file) frames up to 128 bytes; no TCP, no CSMA/CD, no auto-negotiation; receiving needs an external LVDS receiver
JTAG host jtag.pe (TMS sequences, IR/DR shifts of any length, two TAPs in a chain); TAP target jtag_tap.pe; cJTAG OScan1 in front of it test_jtag 6/6, also at gate level; test_jtag_tap 6/6 not run host TCK up to 3.125 MHz, TAP target up to 2.44 MHz; no boundary-scan register; no real adapter yet
SWD host swd_host_gen.pe (DP and AP access, WAIT retries, FAULT and parity reported); target swd_target_gen.pe (MEM-AP model); multi-drop and SWO test_swd 5/5, test_swd_gen 9/9 against an ADIv5 model; test_mem512 one-chip host and target host debugging the target on the same chip; F5t-d66 RTL: an ST-Link V2-1 probe read the target's DPIDR 10 of 10 times at 480 kHz and 5 of 5 at 240 and at 100 kHz (0 of 3 at 950 kHz), and swd_host.pe read the DPIDR of an STM32G474 20 of 20 times, two controls reading no reply; area-reduced RTL (bit cc9fbbae): swd_host_gen.pe powered up the STM32G474's debug domain and read CPUID 0x410FC241 and DBGMCU_IDCODE 0x20036469 through its MEM-AP (docs/swd.md) target up to 1.2 MHz SWCLK in simulation, but the probe on the board failed at 950 kHz (not separated: its sample point or its clock); on the board the probe read only the target's DPIDR; the host's SWCLK on the board was not measured
PS/2 host, device and a loopback on two harts test_ps2 12/12 not run no scan code tables in the device
CAN can_gen.pe: CAN 2.0A/2.0B, bit stuffing, CRC-15 on the chip's CRC unit, arbitration, ACK, error frames, fault confinement, sample point from the data memory test_can_gen 8/8, test_can 5/5, also at gate level; test_can_fc 3/3; test_can_b500 8 tests (and a debug probe) at 500 kbit/s against the KD240's bit time F5t-d66 RTL: 250 kbit/s both ways with the KD240's CAN controller through a transceiver, chip to KD240 50 of 50 frames and KD240 to chip 20 of 20, content exact; TEC, REC, bus-off and overruns 0 (recordprivate repo file); with the SOF fix (fe85164), 500 kbit/s both ways at once against the KD240's 496,031 bit/s: chip to KD240 50 of 50 and KD240 to chip 20 of 20, content exact, TEC, REC and overruns 0, in runs 2 and 3 (run 1 started the KD240 while the chip was being loaded) (recordprivate repo file) both directions up to 500 kbit/s (sending reads each bit back at the sample point, SP 36 to 40 ticks of 50 at 500 kbit/s; the ACK of a received frame from the slot start; a node SOF in bus idle taken at most about 5 ticks late; all since 2026-11-18); no CAN FD
