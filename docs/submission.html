The claim

A protocol chip with a predictive edge launcher: in a device's place it learns the clock of an unmodified host and puts its reply edge at a chosen offset from the predicted clock edge, before or after it, so it can find both ends of the host's sampling window. Around that: four harts of two barrel cores whose instructions are pin operations on an exact clock grid, programs in SRAM loaded over SPI, and calibrated delay lines for steps below one clock. What has prior art and is not claimed as new is listed in novelty.md.

What is not shown

  • The layout of src/ (int8_su) fails three items of the project's own tools/hard_gate.py (6 of 9) while it passes the official flow (run 44564070634: GDS, precheck 10 of 10, gate-level test 19 of 19): the slow corner's max slew at two SRAM pins that only the self test uses (A_BIST_DIN[7] of u_capmem and u_prog1, 0.716 and 0.597 ns against 0.595 ns), and Metal3 at 61.16 % and 60.13 % after two repair steps against the project's own 60 %; setup and hold have no violation. The author decided to submit the version that passes the official criteria (revisions.md, evidence).
  • The official flow passes on the layout of c74b3267, the chip submitted before the shift unit (branch ci/area-decl, run 44417614871): GDS built, precheck 10 of 10, gate-level test 19 of 19; the viewer job failed on the Pages of a private repository (evidence). That layout has the same final netlist and the same SDF of three corners (DATE lines aside) as 2ad37f2 (tools/cmp_final.sh, FINAL SAME), and its own local run passes tools/hard_gate.py 9 of 9, the gate-level test 19 of 19 and the local precheck 10 of 10 (evidence); 2ad37f2 passed the official flow (run 44310801964: GDS, precheck 10 of 10, gate-level test 19 of 19; only the viewer job failed, on the Pages of a private repository) (revisions.md).
  • No committed run of the whole planted bug list on the RTL in src/ (every module passes on it: evidence); tools/ctl_apply.sh only shows that every planted bug still changes its file.
  • No silicon. Real external devices only on the FPGA board, with the RTL of F5t-d66, the chip submitted before the area reduction (bit ad022a41): a PC's USB host, the KD240's CAN controller, a PC network card (10BASE-T transmit), an ST-Link probe and an STM32G474 (SWD); the area-reduced RTL's bit met the first three (README, section 12). Not met: a PC sending to the Ethernet receiver, a JTAG adapter or target, a PS/2 device.
  • Flexibility is shown by history only: the author chose every protocol after the RTL existed. The draw of held-out protocols after a freeze (flexibility.md) has not been run, and the candidate list was visible while the RTL still changed.
  • The flow setup of the passing layouts uses a user SDC that Tiny Tapeout has not accepted. The GitHub gds workflow ran on the two-core RTL: F5t-d66 (run 44219377734) and 2ad37f2 (run 44310801964), each with the gds, precheck and gl_test jobs passing and the viewer job failing (Pages).
  • No line, toggle or FSM coverage numbers; four different protocols on the four harts at once are not tested; the USB result above leaves out the multiplexer, the pads and a host.

The full list: README, Limits and verification.md, section 11.

Reproduce

python3 tools/peasm.py firmware/uart.pe -o $TMPDIR/uart.hex   # 23 words, hart1=12
(cd test && make -B)                                      # test.py, 19 tests, about a minute (Icarus Verilog, cocotb 2.0.1)
python3 tools/ci_shards.py run test_mbist test_dbg        # any modules, each with its own make command
python3 tools/evidence_check.py check                     # recounts the regressions and hashes of evidence/
python3 tools/ledger_check.py check                       # the numbers of the documents against their raw files
python3 tools/doclinks.py                                 # every link and cited path of the documents exists
python3 test/check_asm_compat.py                          # old and new assembler give the same words

ledger_check.py and check_asm_compat.py read files of older commits, so they need a clone with its history (git clone), not a zip or git archive of the tree.

The scripts of those commits have no source gate. The scripts of this tree check that a netlist comes from the src/ they read (tools/hard_gate.py verify-src); for an older netlist they take REPO=<checkout of that commit> and SRC_REV=<that commit>, and read the RTL, tests and firmware of the checkout. A netlist without a stamp is identified only by its MD5: the gate then checks the commit named, not the netlist.

Proofs: the formal/run_formal_*.sh scripts (formal/README.mdprivate repo file). Hardening: the gds workflow, or the flow locally (README, section 13).
