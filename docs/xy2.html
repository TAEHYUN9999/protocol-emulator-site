File Role Words
firmware/xy2.pe Sends X and Y positions to a galvo scanner in XY2-100 frames: CLOCK, SYNC and the two data lines, 20-bit frames (type 001, 16-bit position MSB first, even parity) back to back at 1.923 MHz, one point of a list per frame. 224 of 512 (one hart)

No RTL change. Checked in simulation only. The lines are logic level; the RS-422 drivers are outside the chip.

Sources

XY2-100 has no public standard text. Two public descriptions that agree were used:

Description Used for
sigrok xy2-100 protocol decoder (wiki page and decoders/xy2-100/pd.py, read, not copied) XY2-100 up to 2 MHz (XY2-200 up to 4 MHz); DATA and SYNC taken at the falling edge of CLOCK; SYNC low marks the last bit of a frame; bit layout 0-19 with the type in bits 0-2 (001 = 16-bit position), the position in bits 3-18 MSB first, parity in bit 19, even over the 20 bits
ScannerMAX Mach-DSP user's manual (XY2-100 section) 20 clocks per frame; control bits C2, C1, C0 first, 001 for 16-bit data; inconsistent use of the status line among devices

The decoder and the manual differ on whether the parity is used; the firmware sends it (even), as the decoder checks it.

Frames and timing

The host writes up to 32 points with ETH_W before the run (frame buffer, 4 bytes each: X high, X low, Y high, Y low) and the count N with DATA_W at 0x00; the firmware sends one point per frame, in order, and starts again after the last. Data memory 0x01 PERIOD sets the ticks per bit (0 = 13).

2 MHz would be 12.5 ticks a bit. The 20 bits of a frame are written out: each bit is a twait, the rising edge of CLOCK, the X and Y bits, three slots (SYNC changes at bit 0 and 19), the falling edge 6 ticks after the rising one, and up to 5 instructions that prepare the next point (its bytes in bits 11, 12 and 19, the point after it in bits 13 and 14, the parities with the CRC unit as a CRC-1 in bits 0 to 2). At most 12 instructions sit between two twaits, so 13 ticks (1.923 MHz, 96.2 kHz frames) is the shortest period: the fastest whole-tick rate at or below 2 MHz.

Item XY2-100 Firmware (PERIOD 13)
clock up to 2 MHz 1.923 MHz: high 12 clocks (240 ns), low 14 clocks (280 ns), constant
frame 20 bits, SYNC low on bit 19 20 bits back to back, SYNC low only on bit 19
data at the falling edge steady DATA and SYNC change at most 6 clocks (120 ns) after the rising edge: setup at least 6 clocks (120 ns) before the falling edge, hold at least 16 clocks (320 ns) after it

Tests

test/test_xy2.py, run from a private directory:

T=<repo>/test; R=$T/sim_build/<name>; mkdir -p $R; cd $R
PYTHONPATH=$T make -B -f $T/Makefile PWD=$T SIM_BUILD=$R/build COCOTB_TEST_MODULES=test_xy2

The receiver model takes DATA and SYNC at every falling edge, cuts frames at the SYNC-low bit and checks length, type, even parity and the position; it is written from the two descriptions, not from the firmware.

Controls

Each control is a copy of xy2.pe with one planted bug, run with XY2_FW=<path>. Both fail.

Planted bug Test First failure
Odd parity test_xy2_frames the first frame: type 001 but both parities odd

Limits

  • 1.923 MHz, not the full 2 MHz (12.5 ticks a bit cannot be made from whole ticks without uneven bits).
  • 16-bit positions only (no 18-bit mode, no XY2-100-E commands), no Z channel, no status input.
  • Points come from a list of up to 32 written before the run; the host cannot stream new points while it runs.
