File Role Words
firmware/onewire.pe 1-Wire bus master at standard speed: reset and presence, byte write and read with the chip's CRC-8/MAXIM, and the Search ROM step (triplet) so the host can enumerate every device on the bus. 99 of 512

This protocol was not part of the design when the RTL was written. It uses only existing instructions (low/rel, shout.lsb.od, shin.lsb, tset/twait, crc.lsb, host FIFO). It was added on the RTL of commit a2aad45 without changing it; the 512-word program memory added later (f08b33c) kept the instruction format, so the program assembles to the same words and its tests pass on the current RTL too. Checked in simulation only, not on a board.

Pins

Pin Use
uio[0] (P0) DQ, open drain: driven low or released. Needs an external pull-up (4.7 k typical).

Timing

Maxim AN126 standard speed, in 40 ns ticks. Two values differ from the AN126 table on purpose:

AN126 Firmware Why
A, low before a 1 or a read 6 us 6 us
C, write 0 low 60 us 61 us a device may sample as late as 60 us after the fall
D, recovery after a write 10 us 10 us
E, release to sample 9 us 8 us the sample lands at about 14.2 us, inside the 15 us a device holds a 0
F, rest of a read slot 55 us 55 us
H, reset low 480 us 480 us
I, release to presence sample 70 us 70 us
J, rest of the reset 410 us 410 us

Host interface (FIFO)

Command Bytes Answer
Reset 0x01 0x00 presence seen, 0x01 none
Write 0x02 n b1..bn (n 1 to 255) none
Read 0x03 n (1 to 255) the n bytes, then the CRC-8/MAXIM of them: 0 when the last byte is the CRC of the ones before (ROM, scratchpad)
Search step 0x04 d {direction, complement, id} in bits 2, 1, 0

The search step reads a ROM bit and its complement and writes the direction: the id bit if the two differ, d if both are 0 (devices disagree), 1 if both are 1 (no device left). The host runs the search algorithm of Maxim AN187 on top of it (search() in the test). The CRC runs in the hart's CRC register (crc.lsb, polynomial 0x8C, init 0), so the host does not compute it.

Tests

test/test_onewire.py (cocotb). The device model is written from the DS18B20 datasheet and Maxim AN126 / AN187, not from the firmware. It is strict where the datasheet gives a range: a write slot is sampled at both 15 us and 60 us and the two must agree, a 0 sent by a device is held for only 15 us, and the presence pulse is tried at all four corners of tPDHIGH (15 to 60 us) and tPDLOW (60 to 240 us). Several models share the bus as a wired-AND. A monitor checks the chip's own low times against the datasheet slot table. Run from a private directory:

T=<repo>/test; R=$T/sim_build/<name>; mkdir -p $R; cd $R
PYTHONPATH=$T make -B -f $T/Makefile PWD=$T SIM_BUILD=$R/build COCOTB_TEST_MODULES=test_onewire
Test What Result (2026-11-10)
test_ow_presence No device, then one device at each of the four presence corners, then none again PASS: answers 1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 1
test_ow_rom_and_scratchpad Read ROM of the Maxim AN27 example ROM (02 1C B8 01 00 00 00, CRC A2), Skip ROM + Read Scratchpad, Write Scratchpad and read back, one bit flipped on the wire PASS: ROM right with chip CRC 0, scratchpad CRC 0, flipped bit gives chip CRC 0x37
test_ow_search Three devices whose ROMs differ only in late bits; AN187 search over the search step, then Match ROM + Read Scratchpad for each PASS: all three found, each answers alone

Master low times seen by the monitor in every test: reset 480.08 us, write 0 61.16 us, write 1 and read 6.08 us; no slot shorter than 61 us, no recovery under 1 us.

Controls (test_ow_rom_and_scratchpad with a changed firmware, all three fail):

Change Caught by
E = 12 us (sample about 18 us after the fall) the device's 0 is gone: Read ROM returns all 0xFF
C = 52 us (write 0 low about 58 us) the device model: DQ 0 at 15 us but 1 at 60 us
CRC polynomial 0x31 instead of the reflected 0x8C scratchpad CRC not 0. The AN27 ROM alone does not show it: its residue is 0 under both polynomials.

Rerun of the whole module on commit b4edcc2 (2026-11-11 22:13, Icarus Verilog 11.0, cocotb 2.0.1, RTL, two simulations sharing the machine): test_onewire TESTS=3 PASS=3 FAIL=0 SKIP=0, 889 s wall; every test listed above passed.
